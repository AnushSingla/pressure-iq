03 / PRESSURE EXPLORER

Pressure is a context.

We reconstruct the match state before each delivery. A delivery qualifies when one of the defined high-pressure conditions is met.

SCENARIO 01

Difficult chase

Required run rate of 9 or more. Captures situations where the batting side must accelerate to stay in the contest.

SCENARIO 02

Chase under collapse

Five or more wickets lost with 60 or fewer balls remaining.

SCENARIO 03

First-innings collapse

Five or more wickets lost with 30 or fewer balls remaining.

Expected-performance baseline

Peer comparisons consider format, innings, phase, wickets lost, required-run-rate category and batting-position group. Detailed context groups require at least 100 balls; smaller groups fall back to broader format/innings/phase peers. A player's own deliveries are excluded.

This is an empirical context-matched comparison, not a trained machine-learning prediction model. The report specifies percentile scoring and reliability adjustment towards 50, but not the exact normalization equations.
